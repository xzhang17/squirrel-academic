Squirrel academic profile and custom nightly on macOS

This public snapshot targets Squirrel nightly d220b92 with Rime 1.17.0 and the arm64 smart-spacing extension 1.2. The v1.1.1 profile release links to both the customized app archive and the matching original nightly installer from v1.1.0.

On a new Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later, open Squirrel-Academic-v1.2.0-macOS-arm64.pkg from release v1.2.0, click Continue and Install, and approve macOS authentication. The package installs the customized app, academic profile and clickable indicator, and requests activation of Squirrel - Simplified.

For a manual installation from the separate profile ZIP, open Install.command from that ZIP. Then run:
  /bin/bash "$HOME/Library/Rime/native_typing/Install.command"
Complete the macOS administrator prompt. This installs the saved custom executable without downloading a different release. Choose Squirrel - Simplified and Deploy. The menu indicator can be restored with Mode-Indicator/Restore.command from the release.

New sessions start in English; Shift alone switches Chinese/English. The candidate menu has seven places, with - and = for paging. While composing, [ opens extended lookup and ] returns. Tab moves one syllable forward. Special modifier-key shortcuts are disabled. Full-width mode and automatic bracket completion are absent. In Chinese mode, shifted braces and pipe insert {, }, and | directly; unshifted brackets insert 【 and 】.

Smart spacing supports Chinese/English and Chinese/number boundaries when the editor provides the necessary text context. It does not move the cursor. Number punctuation preserves separators such as 3.14, 12:00 and 1,000.

Normal local Chinese learning is limited to eight characters. Deliberate choices promote phrases to second, then first place. The native learned dictionary is academic_pinyin.userdb; additional selection preferences are in academic_preferences.txt. academic_pref_guard.userdb provides the writer lock. These private records and custom_phrase.txt are not published in this repository.

Add words to the plain dictionaries and shared spellings to word_shortcuts.txt. Keep private phrases in custom_phrase.txt. Choose Deploy after edits. Before manually syncing changed nested resources, run:
  /bin/bash "$HOME/Library/Rime/academic_profile.txt" prepare
Then choose Sync user data. A fresh public installation uses ~/Squirrel-Backup/sync; existing installations retain their own destination. Backups remain manual.

To restore a private backup, run academic_profile.txt restore "$HOME/Library" from its device folder. With Squirrel stopped, keep the current profile aside and put the restored profile at ~/Library/Rime. Reapply native_typing/Install.command to the matching nightly and restore the indicator. The private backup supplies learned vocabulary and private phrases omitted by the public release.

See native_typing/Readme.txt for the saved executable, rebuild command, original-app rollback archive and smart-spacing behavior.
