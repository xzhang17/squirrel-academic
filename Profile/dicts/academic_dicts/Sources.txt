Academic dictionaries: sources and licenses

These dictionaries contain selected subject terminology, common textbook and
Nobel-level scientist names, and mixed Chinese/Latin terms. Cross-disciplinary
entries are assigned to one primary subject. Required source attribution is
kept here; dictionary bodies contain only entries sorted by pinyin/spelling.
The enabled subjects are defined in subjects.yaml.

Selection and pronunciation

Prefer mainland terminology; traditional source spellings were converted with
ICU and selected regional variants normalized or excluded. General fragments
and entries already available in the ordinary Chinese base were excluded from
new selections. Scientist names use customary short forms, with distinct
people sharing a surname distinguished where needed.

Word readings reference Rime Ice and the pinned Wanxiang dictionary. Compound
readings were checked with pypinyin 0.55.0 and reviewed for polyphonic characters.
Mandarin fuzzy spelling is applied by the existing profile; Latin portions of
mixed terms remain exact. Source frequency values were not substituted for
existing Zenfire weights. Curated additions generally use 160 and glossary
additions 100; historical entries retain their existing weights. 简并 uses
12000 to appear below the stronger everyday homophones 兼并 and 煎饼.

Authored selections use the topic/terminology references below. Their
explanatory definitions, teaching prose and software code were not copied.
The vocabulary is curated and does not claim exhaustive subject coverage.
